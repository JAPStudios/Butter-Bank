JAPStudios / Butter Bank

Help Build a Fee-Free Payment System

Butter Bank is developing the concept for a new digital coin and wallet system designed to make everyday payments accessible without transaction fees charged to users.

Contributions will help finance the engineering, research, security testing, and infrastructure needed to develop and evaluate the proposed system.

What Contributions Will Fund

  • Coin and wallet architecture development.
  • Transaction processing and fee-abstraction research.
  • Security audits and testing.
  • Prototype development and documentation.
  • Infrastructure and deployment preparation.

These are intended funding categories. Actual spending and development progress should be reported transparently.

Important Contribution Information

Contributions are for project development. Unless a separate written agreement explicitly states otherwise, contributors should not expect a refund, coin allocation, ownership interest, or financial return.

Cryptocurrency transfers may be irreversible and may incur network fees. Verify the asset, receiving address, and network before sending funds.
