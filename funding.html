What this funding supports

The goal is to develop a coin and wallet experience where users are not charged a transaction fee for ordinary payments. The technical model and funding needed to sustainably cover network and operating costs must be engineered and validated.

  • Coin and wallet architecture, prototypes, and integration.
  • Research into sponsored transactions and fee-abstraction options.
  • Security reviews, testing, and independent audit preparation.
  • Documentation, deployment tooling, and reliability testing.
This is a development proposal, not a claim that a production-ready fee-free network already exists. Blockchain network fees and operating costs may still exist even when the user is not charged directly.
